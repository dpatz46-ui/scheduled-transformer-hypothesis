Preliminary Evidentiary Status

What is presently supported, what remains provisional, and what is not yet claimed

Dustin Patzer · Independent Researcher

Status. Preliminary primary-data work strongly indicates support for the Scheduled Transformer hypothesis. The systematic confirmatory regime is not yet complete.

What the present work indicates

Across several previously collected activation datasets and model families, residual-state trajectories exhibit reproducible depth structure richer than simple monotonic norm growth. Candidate supporting phenomena include:

  • depth-specific radial distributions or bands;
  • substantial changes in distributional width over depth;
  • repeated radial contraction and expansion;
  • persistence and reorganization of relative state positions;
  • candidate context-conditioned departures from a dominant trajectory.

What is already technically validated

The planned full-vector extraction methodology has been smoke-tested on the official base checkpoint of Llama 3.2 3B. For the 28-block model, the extraction preserves 29 persistent residual boundaries together with attention and MLP branch writes. Hook-based reconstruction showed exact captured continuity between adjacent block boundaries and branch reconstruction errors on the order expected from FP16 execution.

What remains provisional

The preliminary datasets were not all acquired under one frozen, uniform protocol. Some are legacy activation archives with different token selections or model-specific boundaries. Context-conditioned sub-schedules, in particular, require deliberate replication under a pre-specified criterion rather than post hoc visual interpretation.

The formal study therefore treats the existing observations as motivating and supporting evidence, not as a substitute for systematic confirmation.

What is not claimed

  • Residual radius is not asserted to encode semantic content by itself.
  • The model is not asserted to explicitly read radius as a numerical depth coordinate.
  • Every context is not expected to produce a distinct radial sub-schedule.
  • The existence of depth-dependent norm growth alone is not claimed as novel.
  • Mechanistic explanations for how or why a schedule forms remain separate from the narrow empirical hypothesis.

Current epistemic posture

The theory is being proposed strongly because it is falsifiable and because preliminary primary-data work indicates that the relevant structure exists. The empirical program is being pursued to determine how broadly that structure replicates, which features are architecture-general, which are task-conditioned, and which preliminary interpretations should be revised.
