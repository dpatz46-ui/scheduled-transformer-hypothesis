Planned Empirical Evaluation

Pre-specified extraction and analysis regime for the Scheduled Transformer hypothesis

Dustin Patzer · Independent Researcher

Primary reference model

The initial clean reference is the official base checkpoint meta-llama/Llama-3.2-3B, a dense pre-RMSNorm decoder model. The validated runtime uses FP16 execution without quantization and eager attention for inspectable module hooks.

Residual-state acquisition

For transformer block l, the planned acquisition preserves:

xlpre,   ΔlA,   xlmid,   ΔlM,   xlout

with the identities:

xlmid = xlpre + ΔlA

xlout = xlmid + ΔlM

xlout = xl+1pre

Full token positions are retained, including beginning-of-sequence position, prefill tokens, and generated tokens. The generated continuation is produced deterministically and then the complete prompt-plus-output sequence is passed once through the causal model with hooks active.

Primary radial observables

ri,p,l = ‖xi,p,l‖,     yi,p,l = log ri,p,l.

The empirical object is the ordered sequence of layer-conditioned distributions, not merely the mean norm curve. Analysis targets centres, widths, shapes, overlap, contraction and expansion, and persistence or reorganization across depth.

Transition diagnostic

With:

yl+1 = yl + dl,

the variance identity is:

Var(yl+1) = Var(yl) + Var(dl) + 2Cov(yl, dl).

Contractive transitions therefore provide a stronger test than simple growth. Where Var(yl+1) < Var(yl), inherited radial position and subsequent radial increment must be sufficiently negatively related.

Context-conditioned sub-schedules

A candidate sub-schedule must be more than a different global slope. The planned operational test removes the dominant depth trajectory together with prompt-specific offset and slope:

ei,l = yi,l − μl − αi − βil.

Candidate sub-schedules are contiguous residual excursions that depart, persist across depth, and potentially reintegrate.

Frozen prompt regime

The planned principal harvest contains 516 prompts:

  • Suite 1 — 260 prompts: 100 recovered matched prompts plus 160 controlled prompts covering relational QA, paraphrase, coherence/nonsense, sequence-position and length structure, and output-length controls.
  • Suite 2 — 256 prompts: flexible-generalization conditions, published and matched arithmetic, natural-language arithmetic, two-hop tasks, mixed-operation chains, and explicit legacy arithmetic probes.

For tasks with known answers, expected answer, actual continuation, and correctness are preserved so analysis can distinguish a task-evoked trajectory from successful task execution.

Validation and falsification pressure

Large-scale extraction proceeds only after residual reconstruction succeeds. The analysis is intended to test whether the proposed schedule replicates across token positions, prompts, and model families, and whether apparent sub-schedules survive controls for simple location, slope, lexical identity, and positional structure.

A useful outcome includes revision or narrowing of the theory. Failure to find reproducible regime structure beyond known mean growth, failure of candidate contraction/reorganization patterns to replicate, or collapse of proposed sub-schedules under appropriate controls would count against stronger formulations.
